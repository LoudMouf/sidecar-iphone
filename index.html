Install on iPhone
  1. Open this link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (you may need to scroll the menu).
  3. If shown, leave Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.

This adds Sidecar to your Home Screen. To give an individual app its own icon, open that app in Safari and repeat these steps. No App Store account or APK download is needed.

Offline access and your data

Open each app online at least once before using it offline. Some features may still need an internet connection. Home Screen installation does not automatically download all three apps.

Entries are stored on this device, not synced between friends. Browser and Home Screen storage can differ. Export important records from the apps where available; clearing website data can delete them. No personal records are bundled with this shelf.

You can open all three apps without installing the shelf.

Made for iPhone, also usable in a desktop browser.
